## How to log a video

1. Create a note in `10 Video Log` named `YYYY-MM-DD - <Title>`.
2. Insert [[Templates/Video Log Template]].
3. Link the script note, paste the final caption, note the AI-label status.
4. Update metrics at 24h, 72h and 7d.
5. Copy the best quoted lines from comments into [[Merch Concepts]].
