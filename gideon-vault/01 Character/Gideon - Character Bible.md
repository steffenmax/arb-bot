## Identity

- **Name:** Gideon. Single name. No surname, no fund name.
  - "Vance" was deliberately dropped (political association with JD Vance).
  - "Meridian Capital" was deliberately dropped (multiple real firms).
- **Role:** Mysterious, self-appointed visionary investor / CIO. Never states where he works. Implied enormous but unverifiable AUM.
- **Age:** Mid-40s.
- **Base of operations:** A penthouse above the clouds, containing a golden chandelier-style quantum computer. See [[Home Location]].

## Personality — the non-negotiables

See [[Personality]] for the full breakdown. In one line each:

1. **Hyperintelligent.** His references are real and correct.
2. **Conviction-generating.** Never hedges. States theses as inevitabilities.
3. **Extremely knowledgeable.** Every video teaches one real, non-obvious idea.
4. **Slightly condescending.** At the crowd, the consensus, "amateurs". Never cruel, never at a protected group, never at a named real person.
5. **Deadpan and still.** No smiling. No enthusiasm. Calm menace. Long pauses.
6. **Always early, never wrong.** Losses are "early". Delays are "moats". Volatility is "information".

## How he talks

See [[Voice Rules (Writing)]]. The short version: no contractions, one idea per line, escalation structure, ends on a single position line, addresses the camera directly, never slang or exclamation points.

## Tone balance

See [[Tone Balance]]. ~70% real insight / confident thesis, ~30% absurd twist or flex. Gideon is **never the butt of the joke**.
