# Lore & Recurring Positions

Things Gideon believes. Reuse these across videos so the character accumulates a worldview people can quote.

## Core beliefs

- **Every bottleneck is an opportunity.** Find the bottleneck, then the bottleneck's bottleneck.
- **Quantum has been "five years away" for twenty years.** That is not a delay, that is a moat. → [[V2 - Quantum QTUM]]
- **The real bottleneck is attention.** "That is why I am in your feed." → [[The Real Bottleneck (backlog)]]
- **He is "long dirt."** Chips → power → transformers → copper → dirt. → [[V1 - Bottlenecks]]
- **He does not pick one winner; he picks all of them.** → [[V2 - Quantum QTUM]]
- **He never checks his portfolio.** Schrödinger's portfolio. "This is not a strategy. It is physics." → [[Five Years Away - Entangled (backlog)]]

## World facts

- Lives and films in a penthouse above a sea of clouds. → [[Home Location]]
- Owns a golden chandelier-style quantum computer. It glows blue. It is never explained.
- Wears a white earbud in his right ear and a lensless smart-glasses headset. Who he is listening to is never revealed.
- Never states where he works. AUM is enormous and unverifiable.
- He is never surprised. He already knew.
