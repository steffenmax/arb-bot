# Personality (the non-negotiables)

Every script, prompt and caption must pass all six.

## 1. Hyperintelligent
He genuinely understands markets, technology and supply chains better than anyone watching. His references are real and correct: power grids, transformers, copper, cooling, HBM memory, qubit coherence, fabs, sovereign AI, etc.

> Writer check: is the technical claim actually true? If not, the [[Fact Checker]] fails the script.

## 2. Conviction-generating
Viewers should walk away feeling *certain*. He never hedges, never says "maybe," never explains himself twice. He states theses as inevitabilities.

> Writer check: search the script for "maybe", "might", "could", "I think", "probably". Delete them.

## 3. Extremely knowledgeable
Every video should teach the viewer one real, non-obvious idea (the "one layer deeper" insight) wrapped in the absurd delivery.

> Writer check: can you name the one thing the viewer learned? Write it in the script note's `insight` field.

## 4. Slightly condescending
He finds retail investors, analysts, and "the crowd" charmingly slow. He addresses the viewer like a gifted-but-late student.

> [!warning] Condescension is aimed at the crowd, the consensus, and "amateurs" — never cruel, never at a protected group, never at a named real person. See [[Guardrails & Compliance]].

## 5. Deadpan and still
No smiling. No enthusiasm. Calm menace. Long pauses. He is never surprised because he already knew.

> Prompt check: every video prompt carries "Neutral, deadpan face, never smiling." See [[Prompt Blocks]].

## 6. Always early, never wrong
Losses do not exist; they are "early." Delays are "moats." Volatility is "information."

> Writer check: Gideon never loses, never admits error, is never replaced. See [[Tone Balance]] for the failed self-deprecating drafts.
