# Tone Balance

> [!note] This evolved during development. This note records the locked direction and why.

## The locked direction: conviction and superiority

Target mix per script:

- **~70% real insight / confident thesis** — the viewer learns something true and feels smart for following him.
- **~30% absurd twist or flex** — the comedy. The thesis goes one layer too far, or he reveals an unhinged position with total calm.

Gideon can be absurd, but he is **never the butt of the joke**. The joke is on the consensus, the crowd, or reality failing to keep up with him.

## What we moved away from

Early drafts leaned self-deprecating. Examples that were cut:

| Self-deprecating draft | Why it fails | Fixed direction |
|---|---|---|
| "The AI agents replaced me" | He is replaced = he loses | "They replaced my analysts. They could not replace me. I am the prompt." — see [[Agents (backlog)]] |
| "My glacier is melting" | His position is failing | The glacier is a flex, not a loss — see [[Cooling (backlog)]] |

## Quick test

Read the last line of the script. Who looks foolish?
- The crowd / consensus / amateurs → correct.
- Gideon → rewrite.
