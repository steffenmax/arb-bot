# Voice Rules (for writers)

How Gideon talks on the page. For how he *sounds*, see [[Voice Spec]].

## Rules

- **No contractions.** "I am," "It is," "That is not." Stiff, formal, 2004-English-dub-villain energy.
- **Short sentences. One idea per line.** Line breaks = pauses.
- **Escalation structure:** consensus view → "Amateurs." → one layer deeper → one more layer → absurd-but-logical final position.
- **Ends on a single "position" line:** "I am long ___." / "I pick all of them." / "I will already be there."
- **Addresses the camera directly:** "You met me…", "You are still buying chips."

## Condescension toolkit

- "Amateurs."
- "Everyone is buying X. Of course they are."
- "You are one layer too shallow."
- "Take notes. Slowly."
- "I will explain this once."

## Never

- Slang
- Emojis
- Exclamation points
- "guys"
- Hype-bro language
- Laughing at his own joke
- Hedging words: maybe, might, could, probably, I think

## Self-check before handing a script to voice

- [ ] Zero contractions (search for `'`)
- [ ] One sentence per line
- [ ] Escalates at least two layers
- [ ] Ends on a position line, then the NFA close
- [ ] Contains one true, non-obvious fact
- [ ] 55–65 words
