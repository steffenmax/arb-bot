# Home Location (default set)

Penthouse at the top of a skyscraper above a sea of clouds.

![[gideon_balcony_still_v1.jpg]]
*First balcony still. Correct: inside the railing, hands on the glass, golden light, city tops in fog. Use as the set reference.*

## Description block (paste into prompts)

```
Wide outdoor balcony at the top of a skyscraper above a sea of clouds, with a solid floor and a waist-high glass railing. Gideon stands on the inside of the railing, on the balcony floor. Behind him, open floor-to-ceiling glass doors lead into a dark, luxurious office containing a large golden chandelier-style quantum computer made of stacked gold plates and coiled copper tubes, glowing softly blue. Flat low-resolution painted cloud skybox, low-poly skyscraper tops in fog, late-afternoon golden light from the left, warm bloom, lens flare.
```

## Blocking rules

> [!warning] Gideon always stands on the INSIDE of the railing.
> The model put him on the wrong side when blocking was vague. Always state where he stands relative to the railing, the doors, and the quantum computer.

- One continuous scene per video: same location, same time of day, same lighting. A cut is a new angle, never a new world.
- Keep movement minimal while speaking. Walking + lip-sync is the riskiest combo.

## Set inventory

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Balcony | Solid floor, waist-high glass railing |
| Doors | Open floor-to-ceiling glass into the office |
| Office | Dark, luxurious |
| Quantum computer | Large, golden, chandelier-style: stacked gold plates, coiled copper tubes, soft blue glow |
| Sky | Flat low-res painted cloud skybox |
| Distance | Low-poly skyscraper tops in fog |
| Light | Late-afternoon golden, from the left, warm bloom, lens flare |

## Suggested angles (3–4 per video)

1. Medium shot, balcony, clouds behind, railing at waist height in frame.
2. Close-up on face, prism glowing, sky bokeh.
3. Over-the-shoulder from inside the office looking out past the quantum computer to Gideon on the balcony.
4. Low angle from the balcony floor, Gideon framed against the sky and the top of the doors.
