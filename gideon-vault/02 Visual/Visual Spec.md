## Look

- **Style:** 2004 PlayStation 2 pre-rendered cutscene. Faceted low-poly hair and skin, smooth waxy shading, subtle polygon edges, mild texture blur, warm bloom. Not photorealistic.
- **Hair:** Silver, slicked back, modeled as blocky faceted polygon clumps; darker gray at temples; ONE thick angular strand falling across the forehead (signature silhouette).
- **Face:** Lean, angular, sharp jaw, high cheekbones, piercing gray eyes, neutral deadpan.
- **Outfit:** Navy quarter-zip pullover, zipped halfway, over a black turtleneck. Slim black trousers, black penny loafers, silver watch on left wrist. No logos.
- **Tech accessories:** White wireless earbud in RIGHT ear. Minimalist smart-glasses headset (Google Glass Enterprise style): thin silver frame, no lenses, matte black module on RIGHT temple, small camera at front hinge, clear glass display prism above the right eye.

## Style rules learned

See [[Style Lessons]] for the full list. Top three:

1. Photoreal drift is the #1 failure mode. Always include "Not photorealistic" and "2004 PlayStation 2 pre-rendered cutscene."
2. Anime-leaning prompts drifted toward a spiky-haired game villain. Avoid spikes; keep it slicked-back finance hair.
3. Never name existing games/shows/characters in prompts.
