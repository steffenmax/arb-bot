# Script Format

**Target:** 55–65 words ≈ 29 seconds. Create new scripts from [[Script Template]].

## Structure

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0–4s | "You met me at a very ___ time in my life." Blank = on-theme adjective (see the bank in [[Catchphrases]]) |
| 2. Consensus | 4–8s | What everyone else believes / is buying |
| 3. Dismissal | — | "Amateurs." or equivalent condescension |
| 4. One layer deeper | 8–20s | The real insight, escalating in short lines |
| 5. Position | 20–25s | The absurd-but-logical final conviction |
| 6. Close | 25–29s | "Gideon. This is not financial advice." |

## Rules

- No contractions
- One sentence per line
- "…" and line breaks for pauses
- 55–65 words
- Must contain **one genuinely true, non-obvious fact** (the [[Fact Checker]] verifies it)
- Must end on a quotable line
- Conviction tone ≥70% (see [[Tone Balance]])
- Prefer themes over tickers. If a ticker is named, NFA goes in the caption too. See [[Guardrails & Compliance]].

## Worked example — [[V2 - Quantum QTUM]]

```
You met me at a very superpositioned time in my life.          ← Hook
They say quantum is five years away… They have said that for twenty years.   ← Consensus
That is not a delay. That is a moat.                            ← Dismissal + layer 1
I do not pick one quantum company. I pick all of them.          ← Layer 2
Q. T. U. M.                                                     ← Position
When the qubits stabilize… I will already be there.             ← Quotable
Gideon. This is not financial advice.                           ← Close
```
