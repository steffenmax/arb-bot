# Mission & Business Goal

**Sole objective of the page:** reach escape velocity on virality so the character itself becomes the asset.

## Monetization paths (rough order of expected arrival)

1. **Merch** — catchphrase apparel. Lowest friction, fully owned. → [[Merch Concepts]]
2. **Sponsored integrations** — AI tools, fintech, trading apps, data/infra startups, and crypto founders who want Gideon to "cover" their project in-character. → [[Guardrails & Compliance]] (paid promotion rules apply)
3. **Partnerships / collabs** — other creators, podcasts, finance/tech brands.
4. **Longer-term** — newsletter ("Gideon's Quarterly Letter"), paid community, licensing the character.

## What success looks like

People quote Gideon lines in comments, duet/stitch him, use his audio, and ask "what is Gideon long on this week?" **The character, not any single video, is the product.**

## Leading indicators to watch (feed the [[Performance Analyst]])

- Comments quoting a line verbatim
- Duets / stitches / audio reuse
- Replies asking for the next position
- Follows per view (character pull, not video pull)
